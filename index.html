Abstract

Fast motion generation without losing the motion.

MixiMotion distills a multi-step HY-Motion-1.0 teacher into a compact, one-step generator. Its progressive curriculum combines bidirectional set matching, decoded motion-quality supervision, semantic balancing, and preference optimization—preserving semantic alignment while making interactive deployment practical.

Method

Offline teacher cache. One-step student.

Teacher motions and text features are cached once. At training time, the student learns to cover diverse teacher candidates; at inference it maps noise and text directly to a motion latent in one evaluation.

Semantic balance

Preference tuning

Group-aware preference pairs select higher-quality teacher motions without online sampling.
